## Lessons this case supports

- **"A day has 24 hours" is a recurring complaint in this community.** Time, for people managing multiple chronic conditions (mental illness, gender dysphoria, work, relationships), is a *scarce resource* in a way it is not for a person with none of those. The phrase is not hyperbole; it is a literal budget report.
- **"Seven positives down to negative" is the most underrated sentence in the diary.** Recovery is a real, measurable thing. The first exam was seven positives; the third was negative. This is *data*. Most discussions of mental illness in this community do not include data; Itamer included it, and that is unusual and worth preserving.
- **"对象" (partner) is a deliberately neutral word.** Itamer never specifies the partner's gender in the diary. The forum community knows; the partner is referred to with male-coded care. Itamer uses 对象 because it does not commit to a public category, and that is the right choice for a public diary.
- **The diary is not "about being trans."** It is about a person with a partner, a violin, a doctor's appointment, a relapse, a box of nicotine patches, a bottle of lotion. The trans identity is *one fact about the person*; the rest of the diary is not about it. This is the right ratio. A community that turns every post into a "trans experience" post loses the actual lives of its members.

## Specific phrases to preserve

- "I wish a day had 48 hours" — appears in multiple entries. The repetition is the point.
- "I need a moment, I trust tomorrow will be better" — last line of day 2. This is the voice of someone who has been through a relapse before and knows that the next morning is the first piece of recovery.
- "Relapsed today" — used as a casual, almost administrative phrase. The casualness is *not* a minimization; it is the tone of someone who has integrated the relapses into a normal week.
