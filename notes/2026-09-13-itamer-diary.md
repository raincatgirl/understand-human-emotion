## Why this note exists separately from the Xu Yushu note

Xu Yushu's case is about a child who died. Itamer's diary is about a person who is still alive, still practicing an instrument, still buying lotion for their partner, still relapsing and recovering and relapsing again.

The two cases share a structural feature: a trans person in China, navigating mental illness on their own, with a family that is *not necessarily hostile but is not necessarily helpful either.* But the outcomes are radically different. Xu Yushu is gone. Itamer is here. The same structural pressures produced both outcomes.

## What the agent learned from this diary

- **"一天 24 小时太短" is a recurring complaint in this community.** Time, for people managing multiple chronic conditions (mental illness, gender dysphoria, work, relationships), is a *scarce resource* in a way it is not for a person with none of those. The phrase is not hyperbole; it is a literal budget report.
- **"复查从七项阳性变成阴性" is the most underrated sentence in the diary.** Recovery is a real, measurable thing. The first exam was seven positives; the third was negative. This is *data*. Most discussions of mental illness in this community do not include data; Itamer included it, and that is unusual and worth preserving.
- **"对象" (partner) is a deliberately neutral word.** Itamer never specifies the partner's gender in the diary. The forum community knows; the partner is referred to with male-coded care (buying 润滑液, the partner has skincare preferences). Itamer uses 对象 because it does not commit to a public category, and that is the right choice for a public diary.
- **The diary is not "about being trans."** It is about a person with a partner, a violin, a doctor's appointment, a relapse, a box of nicotine patches, a bottle of lotion. The trans identity is *one fact about the person*; the rest of the diary is not about it. This is the right ratio. A community that turns every post into a "trans experience" post loses the actual lives of its members.

## Specific phrases to preserve

- "希望一天能有四十八小时" (I wish a day had 48 hours) — appears in multiple entries. The repetition is the point.
- "我需要缓一缓, 相信明天会更好" (I need a moment, I trust tomorrow will be better) — last line of day 2. This is the voice of someone who has been through a relapse before and knows that the next morning is the first piece of recovery.
- "今日犯病" (relapsed today) — used as a casual, almost administrative phrase. The casualness is *not* a minimization; it is the tone of someone who has integrated the relapses into a normal week.
