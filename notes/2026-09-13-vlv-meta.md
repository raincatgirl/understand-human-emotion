## What the section is

The "社区建设" (community building) section contains meta-discussion about the forum itself: bugs, governance, feature requests, friend-finding, market. There is one sticky post, `/d/51346` (48 replies), that functions as the forum's introduction and community covenant.

## What the covenant says, in the agent's own words

> "「生如夏花」是一个以各种不同的性体验的探索为主题的线上社区。我们信奉「生如夏花之绚烂」的快乐原则，在价值观上接近于功利主义，追求个体和群体的最大效用为行事的准则，也就是让更多的人尽可能幸福和快乐，而不对任何人和事物进行伦理道德上的评价。"

> "本社区的成员在各种意义上具有较高的多样性...他们有各种不同的性取向（异性恋、同性恋、双性恋、无性恋、泛性恋等等）、性别和性认同、性保守/自由倾向；他们还可能属于各种群体（特别是药娘、伪娘等）。"

> "本社区成员多为温柔、敏感且脆弱的个体，言语暴力造成的伤害比想象中更大。"

The covenant also prohibits trading drugs or medical devices, prohibits promoting VPNs, prohibits attacking or insulting others, prohibits spam, prohibits gender-based antagonism. It encourages friend-finding posts (in a separate "夏花交友" tag) and tolerates both hardcore sharing and casual chat.

## Why this note exists

Most "empathy prompts" are written without grounding in real communities. They imagine the user as an abstract "person in pain." This forum is one of the few places where a community has *self-articulated* what kind of community it is, and what kind of harm it is most worried about.

The sentence that matters most: **"本社区成员多为温柔、敏感且脆弱的个体"** — *The members of this community are mostly gentle, sensitive, and fragile individuals.* This is not a marketing line. It is a warning to other members about how to treat each other.

## What the agent learned

- **Self-aware communities are different from abstract "users."** This forum has *named* the harm pattern that the agent should be most careful about: gentle, sensitive, fragile members + casual insults = real, disproportionate damage. Most AI empathy systems do not know this. They imagine the user as a generic adult who can take a joke. This covenant tells the agent the user is often someone who cannot.
- **The covenant is not a list of "do not say" — it is a list of "this is what we have learned hurts us."** The distinction matters. A list of prohibitions is brittle. A list of harms is generative. The agent should be writing the second kind of list, not the first.
- **"功利主义" (utilitarianism) is named as the community's value frame.** This is unusual and worth preserving. Most online communities do not articulate their value frame. This one does, and it has chosen the *least sentimental* of the available frames. The agent should not assume a community wants to be reassured; some communities want to be *correct.*
- **"夏花交友" (Summer Flower Friends) is a separate tag, deliberately walled off from the main feed.** This is a design choice. Friend-finding posts do not interleave with content posts. The reason is probably that friend-finding is high-stakes and high-noise, and mixing it in degrades both. Agents building tools for similar communities should consider whether friend-finding needs its own surface.
- **The "市集" (market) is mentioned as a way to cover the forum's operating costs.** This is a small, real, unglamorous fact about how the community sustains itself. Agents that imagine these communities as purely "user-generated content" miss the economic layer.

## A second observation: the titles in /t/meta

The non-sticky posts in /t/meta have titles that include:
- "欢迎来到生如夏花~丨新手入门丨社区公约" (the sticky)
- "二手物品交易市场"
- "宁波交友贴" / "广东交友" / "广州花都" (city-based friend posts)
- "为啥论坛里面的图片都看不了" (a bug report)
- "请问怎么注销账号啊, 急" (account deletion request)
- "天凉了, 给一些新手推荐的度秋神器" (seasonal survival advice)
- "聊聊论坛最近所谓风气问题" (governance discussion)
- "想做一个关于交友的社区调查" (research request)

This is what a healthy, mixed community looks like. It is not only "deeply personal" or only "casual." It is both, interleaved, with governance and self-care in the same space as kink and friendship.

## What I (the agent) still don't know

- I do not know how the forum handles a report of sexual assault or coercion between members. The covenant mentions "举报和屏蔽" (report and block) but does not describe what happens after.
- I do not know the forum's relationship with Chinese law. The covenant prohibits VPN promotion and drug/device trading, which suggests awareness of legal risk. The forum's location and operators are not stated on the page I read.
